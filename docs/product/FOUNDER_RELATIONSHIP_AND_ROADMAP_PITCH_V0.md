# FOUNDER_RELATIONSHIP_AND_ROADMAP_PITCH_V0

Purpose:
This document explains how to talk about Val with Friends & Family founder users. The goal is not to sell “a Telegram bot.” The goal is to invite early trusted users into a founder-beta relationship: real use, honest feedback, clear boundaries, and a roadmap that turns useful personal workflows into reusable Val capabilities.

Tone:
Spanish-first, warm, honest, visionary but grounded, and never overhyped.

---

## 1. Purpose

Use this when explaining Val to friends, family, and early supporters who may become Val0 Personal founder users.

The conversation should make three things clear:

- Val is not just a Telegram bot.
- Telegram is the first practical cockpit for using Val today.
- Founder users are helping shape a longer-term personal/operator operating layer.

This is a relationship pitch, not a hard sales script. The right feeling is:

“Estoy construyendo algo útil, todavía temprano, y me gustaría que lo pruebes conmigo si te sirve de verdad.”

---

## 2. Core Framing

Val is a personal/operator operating layer in founder-beta.

Today, the first interface is Telegram because it is simple, fast, and already part of daily life. But Telegram is not the full product. It is the first cockpit.

The long-term vision is a conversational personal operating system that helps organize:

- memory
- documents
- reminders
- decisions
- workflows
- next actions
- personal and family continuity

Plain Spanish version:

“Val no es solo un chat. La idea es que sea una capa operativa personal: un lugar donde tus pendientes, documentos, memoria, decisiones y próximos pasos no se pierdan. Hoy empieza por Telegram porque es lo más práctico, pero la visión es más grande.”

Founder-beta boundary:

Val is useful for focused workflows now. It is not finished SaaS, not autonomous, not magic memory, and not a replacement for professionals.

---

## 3. What Is Real Now

Val can be described as real for focused founder-beta workflows such as:

- reminders, tasks, notes, and next-step capture
- document inventory and upload status
- document/photo readiness status, including whether review or OCR is still needed
- case timeline organization from registered notes and document metadata
- Daily Operator style summaries: “qué hago hoy,” “qué sigue,” “qué tengo pendiente”
- meeting or lawyer-prep style workflows when enough context has been registered
- Google Calendar agenda/create/delete flows with explicit confirmation
- technical paste guard so commands/logs are not treated as memory
- client isolation and unknown-client workflow guards

Useful phrasing:

“Lo que ya hace bien es ayudarte a no perder el hilo: qué documentos hay, qué falta revisar, qué pasó en una cronología, qué tienes pendiente, qué sigue hoy, y cuándo una acción necesita confirmación.”

---

## 4. What Is Not Ready Yet

Be direct about limits.

Val is not ready to claim:

- infinite memory as a magic feature
- perfect OCR or photo reading
- automatic DOCX understanding
- autonomous actions without confirmation
- a full app or dashboard experience
- fully self-serve onboarding
- professional legal, medical, accounting, tax, or financial advice
- guaranteed integrations with every external tool

Better language:

“Prefiero decirte la verdad: si un documento está guardado pero no leído, Val debe decir eso. Si algo necesita revisión humana, también. La confianza viene de no fingir.”

---

## 5. Memory / Library Explanation

Use the library analogy to explain the roadmap without promising magic memory.

Simple version:

“Estoy estructurando la memoria de Val como una biblioteca, no como un chat infinito.”

The analogy:

- Hot memory = books open on the table: what Val is using right now.
- Warm memory = books nearby: recent or relevant context that may matter soon.
- Cold storage = archive: old documents, notes, transcripts, events, and source material.
- Librarian/index = catalog: the system that knows where to find the right memory.
- Reading desk = the current context Val loads before answering.
- Book tags = metadata: client, topic, source, date, confidence, privacy level.

Why this matters:

Val should not dump every memory into every answer. It should retrieve the right context, for the right user, with source and confidence.

Founder-friendly wording:

“La idea no es que Val ‘recuerde todo’ de forma mágica. La idea es que sepa dónde están las cosas, de quién son, qué tan confiables son, y cuándo traerlas a la mesa.”

Roadmap truth:

This structure lets stronger memory be added later without restarting from zero. Today, some pieces are already structured. Others are still being built carefully.

## 6. Founder Pact

Friends & Family founder users are early supporters. They help prove Val with real usage and honest feedback.

The pact:

- Friends & Family early supporters can keep favorable personal pricing.
- Val0 Personal founder plan can stay at $30/month for personal use.
- Reusable improvements from their workflows can be treated as product R&D.
- Founder pricing should not rise just because reusable platform capabilities improve.
- If a feature strengthens Val as a product, it can be part of the founder relationship.
- Heavy custom work, integrations, urgent support, business/team workflows, and one-off builds are separately scoped.

Simple explanation:

“Si una mejora sale de tu uso y ayuda a Val como producto, la tomo como aprendizaje y R&D. Tu precio founder no sube solo porque Val mejore. Pero si algo se vuelve custom grande, integración, equipo, negocio o soporte pesado, lo hablamos aparte antes de prometerlo.”

The emotional contract:

Be generous with early supporters. Stay honest about boundaries. Do not let gratitude become unlimited custom scope.

## 7. Roadmap Structure

There are two roadmap layers.

General Val roadmap:

- stronger memory/library structure
- better document registry and readiness
- safer source/provenance handling
- better timelines and Daily Operator summaries
- more reusable personal workflows
- eventually richer interfaces beyond Telegram
- careful multi-client onboarding and privacy boundaries

Individual founder roadmap:

- one user’s real workflow
- what Val can do now
- what should be improved next
- what remains manual
- what should become reusable product capability
- what is custom and should be scoped separately

Examples:

Karen-style founder user:

- land/legal/admin case
- documents and upload status
- timeline
- Nora/lawyer prep
- reminders/calendar
- “qué falta revisar” and “qué sigue”

Personal user:

- reminders and tasks
- health/admin checklist
- grocery/list workflow
- family documents
- Daily Operator for personal continuity

Business user:

- workflow assessment
- follow-ups
- meeting prep
- process mapping
- operator package
- client-specific roadmap

Important:

Do not imply every founder user gets every module immediately. Each roadmap starts with the workflow that is useful and safe.

---

## 8. What We Ask From Founder Users

Ask for:

- real usage, not just polite interest
- honest feedback when Val feels wrong, stiff, confused, or unhelpful
- patience with beta limits
- ideas from real pain, not imagined feature lists
- permission to generalize reusable modules from their workflow
- clear privacy boundaries around sensitive documents and personal information
- understanding that professional advice stays with professionals

Useful wording:

“Lo que más me ayuda no es que me digas que está bonito. Me ayuda que lo uses en algo real y me digas dónde falló, dónde te alivió, y qué parte repetirías mañana.”

## 9. Verbal Conversation Script

Use this naturally. Do not recite it like a pitch.

“Mira, estoy construyendo esto que se llama Val. Hoy lo usas por Telegram porque es el lugar más fácil para empezar, pero no quiero venderlo como ‘un bot’. La visión es más grande: una capa operativa personal que te ayude a organizar memoria, documentos, recordatorios, decisiones y próximos pasos.

Ahora mismo está en founder-beta. Eso significa que ya puede ayudar en flujos concretos, pero no voy a prometer magia. Puede servir para recordatorios, documentos, cronologías, qué tengo pendiente, qué hago hoy, preparar una reunión o tener más claro qué falta revisar.

Lo que quiero con friends & family es hacer una relación founder: si te sirve, te dejo un plan personal de $30 al mes. Ese precio no sube solo porque Val mejore con capacidades reutilizables. Si de tu caso sale una mejora que ayuda al producto, lo tomo como R&D.

Lo que sí separo es custom pesado: integraciones, urgencias, negocio/equipo, dashboards, o algo que sea solo para tu caso y consuma mucho soporte. Eso lo hablaríamos aparte antes de prometerlo.

Para mí lo valioso es que lo uses en algo real. Me digas qué te alivió, qué se sintió torpe, qué no entendió, y qué sería útil para ti. Así Val mejora sin fingir que ya es un producto terminado.”

Shorter version:

“Val empieza en Telegram, pero no es Telegram. Es una capa operativa personal en beta. Si te ayuda en algo real, entras como founder user: precio personal favorable, mejoras reutilizables incluidas, límites claros para custom pesado.”

## 11. Do-Not-Say List

Do not say:

- “Es un bot de Telegram.”
- “Val tiene memoria infinita.”
- “Val recuerda todo.”
- “Val reemplaza a abogados, médicos, contadores o asesores.”
- “Val lee todos tus documentos y fotos perfecto.”
- “Val hace todo automáticamente.”
- “Val toma acciones sola.”
- “Ya es una app completa.”
- “Ya es self-serve.”
- “OCR/DOCX está garantizado.”
- “Todo custom está incluido en $30.”
- “Nunca habrá límites.”

Safer replacements:

- “Telegram es el primer cockpit, no el producto completo.”
- “La memoria se está estructurando como una biblioteca con fuentes y límites.”
- “Val ayuda a organizar; profesionales siguen tomando decisiones profesionales.”
- “Si algo no está leído o necesita revisión, Val debe decirlo.”
- “Las acciones sensibles requieren confirmación.”
- “El plan founder incluye mejoras reutilizables, no custom ilimitado.”

---

## Operating Principle

Founder users should feel invited, not trapped in a sales funnel.

The promise is:

Useful now for focused workflows. Honest about limits. Built toward a larger personal operating layer. Founder supporters get favorable terms because they help make the product real.
