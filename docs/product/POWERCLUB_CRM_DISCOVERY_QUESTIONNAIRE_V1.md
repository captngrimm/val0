# POWERCLUB-CRM-BATTLE-01F - Discovery Questionnaire V1

## Purpose

Use this questionnaire before or during the PowerClub meeting to confirm pilot scope, decision-makers, operational pain, and dashboard priorities.

Do not use real PowerClub data in the demo. Discovery can discuss real processes and requirements, but any implementation or data handling must be separately approved and scoped.

## 1. Business / Branch Structure

- How many branches should be considered for the pilot?
- Are branches managed independently or centrally?
- Is there a general manager view across all branches?
- Are there regional managers or only branch/general managers?
- Which branch would be safest for a first pilot?
- Which branch has the clearest follow-up pain?

## 2. Users / Advisors / Operators

- How many advisors/operators would use the pilot?
- How many branch managers need visibility?
- Who needs a general manager view?
- Do advisors work by shift?
- Are leads assigned to one advisor or shared by branch?
- What happens when an advisor is unavailable?

## 3. Current Lead / Socio Flow

- Where do leads come from today?
- How are prospects assigned?
- What happens after first contact?
- What happens after a visit?
- How are renewals/reactivations handled?
- What is the difference between socio status and management/follow-up status?
- Where does follow-up break down most often?

## 4. Current Data Sources / Excel / Export

- Are leads currently managed in Excel, Google Sheets, exports, CRM, or another system?
- How often are files updated?
- Who owns the file/source of truth?
- Are there duplicate records?
- Are historical records important for V1?
- Is import required, or can the pilot start with manual/sample entry?
- Is export required for reporting?

## 5. Statuses And Follow-Up Rules

- What statuses are used today?
- Which statuses are actually useful for management?
- What counts as contacted?
- What counts as interested?
- What counts as converted/sale?
- What counts as lost?
- When is a follow-up due?
- When is it overdue?
- Which leads are hot/warm/cold?
- What makes an opportunity stuck?

## 6. Current Pain Points

- Where are opportunities most likely to be lost?
- What does management not see until too late?
- Which tasks depend on advisor memory?
- Which reports take too long to prepare?
- Which branch or process causes the most friction?
- What creates the most manual work?

## 7. Manager Reporting Needs

- What does the general manager need to see daily?
- What does a branch manager need to see daily?
- What needs to be reviewed weekly?
- Which numbers are reviewed at month-end?
- Which report exists today but is hard to trust?
- Which report does not exist but would change decisions?

## 8. Dashboard Priorities

Rank these manager views:

- Follow-up Control View.
- Advisor Performance View.
- Branch Comparison View.
- Recovery Opportunities View.

Questions:

- If only one dashboard view ships first, which one matters most?
- Which widget would management check every morning?
- Which widget would make the pilot feel financially useful?
- Which dashboard view would be dangerous or misleading if data quality is weak?

## 9. Import / Export Needs

- Does V1 need import?
- Does V1 need export?
- Which file format matters?
- Who prepares imported data?
- Who checks data quality?
- Is Excel output required or just nice to have?
- Are exports for managers, advisors, accounting, or leadership?

## 10. Training / Rollout Constraints

- Who needs training first?
- How many training sessions are realistic?
- Are advisors available at the same time?
- Does training happen by branch?
- Who enforces usage?
- What would cause advisors not to use the pilot?
- What support is needed during the first week?

## 11. Security / Privacy Expectations

- Who should see all branches?
- Who should see only one branch?
- Should advisors see only assigned records?
- Is production authentication required for the pilot?
- What data should never be shown in demo materials?
- Are there privacy rules around phone/email/member data?
- Who approves data access?

## 12. Scope Freeze Questions

- Which branches are included?
- How many advisors/users are included?
- What monthly record volume should we expect?
- Which fields are required?
- Which statuses are approved?
- Which follow-up rules are approved?
- Which dashboard widgets are included?
- Which reports are included?
- What is explicitly excluded?
- Who approves change requests?

## 13. Success Criteria

- What would make the pilot successful after 30 days?
- What would make it successful after 60 days?
- What decision should PowerClub be able to make after 90 days?
- Which metric proves improved visibility?
- Which manager behavior should change?
- Which advisor behavior should change?

## 14. Budget / Timeline Readiness

- Is this exploratory or budget-ready?
- Is there a target launch date?
- Is a three-month pilot acceptable?
- Who approves setup cost?
- Who approves maintenance?
- Is a reduced pilot needed, or is the recommended pilot viable?
- What budget range is realistic?

## 15. Decision-Maker / Stakeholder Mapping

- Who is the business owner?
- Who is the general manager stakeholder?
- Who represents branch managers?
- Who represents advisors/operators?
- Who represents IT/admin?
- Who approves budget?
- Who signs scope freeze?
- Who can say no?

## 16. Questions Frank Should Ask Karen Before The Boss Meeting

- Who will be in the meeting?
- What does the boss care about most: sales, follow-up, branch control, reporting, or cost?
- Is the current pain more advisor discipline or management visibility?
- Which branch/process is safest to mention as pilot candidate?
- Are there sensitive topics to avoid?
- What language does PowerClub use: socios, leads, prospectos, asesores, operadores?
- Is there already a CRM/system they dislike?
- Should pricing be discussed directly or only after discovery?
- What would make the meeting feel successful to her?

## 17. Questions For General Manager

- What do you wish you could see every morning?
- Where do you think opportunities are being lost?
- Which branch needs the most visibility?
- Which follow-up report is hardest to trust?
- How do you currently compare advisor performance?
- What decision would this dashboard need to support for you?
- If this pilot worked, what would you expand first?

## 18. Questions For Operations / Sales Manager

- How are advisors assigned leads today?
- How do advisors know what to follow up today?
- What happens when follow-up is late?
- How are visits tracked?
- What does "lost" mean in practice?
- What causes no-contact or stuck opportunities?
- What training would advisors need?
- What would make the workflow too heavy?

## 19. Questions For IT / Admin If Present

- What systems currently hold lead/socio data?
- Are exports available?
- Are there data access restrictions?
- What format can be shared for a pilot?
- Is authentication required for any live pilot?
- Are there security/privacy requirements?
- Who would support data import/export?
- Are integrations off-limits in V1?

## 20. Do Not Ask Too Early

Avoid asking these before pain and scope are clear:

- "Can we connect WhatsApp?"
- "Can we connect payments?"
- "Can we get all historical data?"
- "Can we build a mobile app?"
- "Can Val answer everything automatically?"
- "Can we replace your current CRM?"
- "What exact production SLA do you need?"

Reason:

```text
These questions pull the meeting toward heavy implementation before the pilot value is agreed.
```

## 21. Red Flags

Watch for:

- They want all branches immediately but have no pilot owner.
- They want WhatsApp/payment integrations in V1.
- They expect unlimited reports.
- They cannot define statuses.
- They cannot identify a decision-maker.
- They want a final CRM without discovery.
- They expect maintenance to be free.
- They want Val Copilot included as full autonomous assistant.
- They do not want to freeze scope.

Response:

```text
That may be possible later, but it is outside a controlled V1 pilot. First we should freeze one useful visibility workflow and prove it.
```

4. Complete scope freeze checklist.
5. Select pricing tier.
