# PRICING_AND_FOUNDER_PLAN_BOUNDARIES_V0

Purpose:
Define pricing and boundary rules for Val0 Personal founder users, Friends & Family founder supporters, and Val1 Business packages.

The goal is to be generous with early supporters without accidentally promising infinite custom work.

---

## Val0 Personal Founder Plan

Price:

- $30/month flat

Best for:

- individual/personal use
- family/admin continuity
- personal documents, reminders, tasks, and next steps
- Karen-style workflows
- Telegram-first daily use while Val grows into a broader personal operating layer

Includes:

- Val access through the current Telegram cockpit
- available Val0 Personal modules
- notes, reminders, tasks, and idea capture where enabled
- document inventory/status where enabled
- timeline/Daily Operator-style personal organization where enabled
- general reusable improvements to existing modules
- light configuration and ambienting
- founder-beta guidance and feedback loop

This price is for a personal operator relationship, not a custom software retainer.

Telegram is the first practical interface. The product direction is bigger: a conversational personal operating layer for memory, documents, reminders, workflows, decisions, and next actions.

---

## Friends & Family Founder Rule

Early supporters may keep favorable pricing if they help prove the product.

This is acceptable because founder users give:

- real usage patterns
- trust feedback
- routing failures
- language/tone feedback
- module ideas
- proof that the product solves painful workflows

Reusable module development can be treated as product R&D, not billed custom, when it can later help other users or clients.

Founder pricing should not rise simply because reusable platform capabilities improve. Early supporters helped prove the product, so general product evolution is part of the founder relationship.

Examples of acceptable product R&D under a founder personal plan:

- land/legal timeline module
- document organization pattern
- document upload status improvements
- Daily Operator improvements
- safer meeting/lawyer prep package patterns
- grocery/list improvements that become reusable list/inventory modules
- client-isolation and unknown-client guardrails

The key test:

If the improvement strengthens the platform or can become a reusable module, it can fit inside founder-plan learning.

If the request is only a one-off custom demand with high support burden, it should be separately scoped.

---

## What $30 Does Not Include

The Val0 Personal founder plan does not include:

- unlimited human support
- urgent custom development
- guaranteed response times
- complex integrations
- large migrations
- bulk document cleanup
- guaranteed OCR/photo reading
- guaranteed DOCX automation
- infinite memory claims
- enterprise/team workflows
- multi-user permissions
- dashboards/client portals
- external SaaS setup
- custom CRM/ERP/accounting integration
- legal, medical, accounting, tax, or financial professional advice
- sensitive/high-risk automation without a separate scope

Simple rule:

$30/month buys access to the personal founder-beta operator and the reusable product path. It does not buy unlimited custom engineering.

Val1 is the business route.

Includes:

- 45-60 minute workflow assessment
- pain-point mapping

- first workflow recommendation
- month 1 roadmap
- privacy and risk boundaries
- what can be done now vs later

Best for:

- business owners
- operators
- consultants
- professionals with messy processes
- anyone who may need a business workflow package but is not ready to commit monthly

Purpose:
Provide a founder-operated AI workflow implementation lane.

Includes:

- guided setup
- Val access through the current Telegram cockpit
- 1-3 active workflows
- process mapping
- client-specific roadmap
- workflow ownership
- weekly or biweekly check-in
- light refinement and maintenance
- quality checks before changes

Separately scoped:

- custom integrations
- dashboards
- team workflows
- data migrations

## Upgrade Triggers

Move from Val0 Personal to Val1 Business or a custom scope when any of these appear:

- multiple users or team access
- business process ownership
- customer/vendor/revenue workflow
- higher support burden
- urgent turnaround expectation
- custom integrations
- large document/data migration
- sensitive or high-risk workflow
- regulatory, legal, financial, medical, or accounting implications
- SLA/uptime expectations
- dashboard/client portal requirement
- recurring founder/operator time beyond light support

If the work starts looking like operations consulting or custom implementation, it is not a $30 personal plan anymore.

## Simple Explanation

Use this:

“Same core system, different scope and support level.”

Val0 Personal:

- one person/family
- personal/admin continuity
- Telegram as first cockpit
- lower price
- reusable improvements included when they strengthen the product
- founder price protected as the reusable platform improves

Val1 Business:

- business workflow
- process mapping
- more support
- roadmap ownership
- implementation help
- higher base price

## Guardrails

Protect founder users:

- honor favorable pricing for early supporters where possible
- keep improving reusable modules based on their real usage
- do not raise founder personal pricing just because platform-level reusable capabilities improve
- do not nickel-and-dime every product-learning improvement

Protect the business:

- do not promise infinite custom work
- do not accept urgent custom scope inside the $30 plan
- do not let special favors destroy sustainability
- do not build one-off features unless they are clearly worth it
- move business workflows into Val1 when support/process load increases

Product rule:

Development should remain reusable wherever possible.

If a request improves the platform, it can be product R&D.

If a request only serves one client’s edge case and requires meaningful engineering/support, scope it separately.

## Example Language For Karen / Friends

Use this tone: grateful, clear, and firm.

Example:

“Because you are one of the first people helping me prove Val, I want to keep you on a simple founder personal plan: $30/month. That includes the personal Val experience and improvements to modules that can become reusable, like documents, timeline, reminders, and Daily Operator.”

Boundary:

“If something becomes a big custom build, urgent support, an integration, or a business/team workflow, I’ll tell you before treating it as included. I want to be generous, but I also need to keep Val sustainable.”

Short version:

“You keep the founder personal price. Reusable product improvements are included as Val grows beyond the first Telegram cockpit. Big custom edge cases get scoped separately.”

---

## Decision Rule

When a request comes in, ask:

1. Is this personal or business?
2. Is it one user or multiple users?
3. Is this reusable product improvement or one-off custom work?
4. Does it touch sensitive/high-risk data or professional advice?
5. Does it require urgent support or strong reliability?
6. Does it require integration with external tools?

If mostly personal + reusable + low support:
keep in Val0 Personal founder plan.

If business + process ownership + support/integration:
move to Val1 Business or custom scope.

---

## Operating Principle

Be generous with founder supporters, but do not confuse gratitude with unlimited scope.

The founder plan should create product learning and loyalty. It should not become a trap where every custom request is silently included forever.
