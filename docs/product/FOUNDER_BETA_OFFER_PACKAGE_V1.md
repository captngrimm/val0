# Founder Beta Offer Package V1

Purpose: package Val0 as an honest founder-beta offer that can be explained after the demo.

## Who This Is For

Val0 founder beta is for people who have important information scattered across Telegram, WhatsApp, notes, screenshots, documents, reminders, calendar events, or memory.

Good fit:

- a person managing family/admin continuity
- a professional with document or follow-up clutter
- a founder or operator tracking leads, tasks, and decisions
- someone with one messy workflow that would benefit from a guided Telegram operator

Not a good fit yet:

- someone expecting a finished public SaaS dashboard
- someone needing guaranteed OCR/legal/accounting/medical accuracy
- someone wanting full autonomy without review
- a team needing permissions, SLA, or enterprise integrations today

## The Offer In Simple Human Terms

Val0 is a personal/operator assistant that starts with one useful workflow and grows from real usage.

Simple Spanish:

> Escogemos un workflow que te está consumiendo cabeza, configuramos Val alrededor de eso, y corremos una primera semana controlada. Val ayuda a recordar, organizar, resumir y preparar próximos pasos, pero no reemplaza criterio humano ni profesionales.

Simple English:

> We pick one workflow that is causing friction, configure Val around it, and run a controlled first week. Val helps remember, organize, summarize, and prepare next steps, but it does not replace human judgment or professional review.

## Boundaries And Trust

Val0 should be sold as private, careful, and human-in-the-loop.

Use this language:

> Val helps organize your information and next steps. It does not replace a lawyer, doctor, accountant, financial advisor, or professional reviewer. Sensitive actions and conclusions stay under human control.

Trust rules:

- start with one workflow
- do not promise perfect memory
- do not promise perfect OCR
- do not promise full autonomy
- keep client data scoped carefully
- confirm sensitive writes/actions
- explain what is beta vs ready

## Call To Action

Ask:

> What is one workflow you are tired of holding in your head?

Then propose:

1. Pick one workflow.
2. Do a guided setup.
3. Run a controlled first week.
4. Review what helped, what failed, and what should expand.
