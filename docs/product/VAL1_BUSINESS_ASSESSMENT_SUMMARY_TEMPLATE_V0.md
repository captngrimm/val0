# VAL1_BUSINESS_ASSESSMENT_SUMMARY_TEMPLATE_V0

Purpose:
Reusable template for the deliverable after a $150 Val1 Business assessment.

This is an assessment summary template, not an implementation contract, not an automation promise, not legal/financial/medical/accounting/tax/professional advice, and not runtime/client setup. Use it to summarize what was learned, recommend a scoped next step, and keep boundaries clear.

Tone:
Spanish-first, clear, business-readable, honest, no hype.

### Data Hygiene

- Do not store private business data in product docs.
- Keep source notes in the approved assessment/client location.
- Sanitize product learnings before moving them into reusable docs.
- Do not create runtime behavior, client routes, integrations, or real client folders from this template alone.
- Keep Val1 as the business offer route on the same Val operating layer, not a separate platform.

Operating principle:
This summary should make the next step obvious without overpromising. Clarity first, automation later if it is safe and scoped.
