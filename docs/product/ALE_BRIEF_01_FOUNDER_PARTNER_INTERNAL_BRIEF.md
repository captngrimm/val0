# ALE-BRIEF-01 Founder/Partner Internal Brief

Purpose: give Frank a clear internal brief for explaining Val to Ale as a trusted founder-beta supporter and possible strategic partner, not as a cold sales target.

This is docs-only. It does not add runtime behavior, persistence, profile writes, calendar/reminder/task actions, database migrations, production config, or client data changes.

## 1. Purpose

ALE-BRIEF-01 helps Frank walk into the Ale conversation with the right posture:

- warm, clear, and grounded
- honest about what Val already does
- honest about what does not exist yet
- focused on one useful workflow first
- open to Ale as both user and strategic mirror

The goal is not to impress Ale with every possible feature. The goal is to show the Personal OS direction through one real first workflow and invite her feedback as someone Frank trusts.

## 2. Who Ale Is In This Context

Ale is:

- a trusted warm person
- a trusted partner in the founder-beta conversation
- a potential first serious founder-beta supporter
- a possible strategic partner / right hand
- someone who can help Frank see what is useful, awkward, unclear, or emotionally right
- not a cold sales target
- not someone to pressure into buying

Frank's posture:

```text
I am not trying to close Ale like a prospect. I am inviting a trusted person into the early shape of Val so we can test whether it helps in one real part of her life/work.
```

## 3. What Val Is

Val is a personal operator / cognitive co-pilot.

She starts in Telegram for now, but Telegram is the current surface, not the full product idea.

Val is designed to:

- turn messy life/work context into workflows
- help organize agenda, tasks, reminders, documents, ideas, and follow-up
- reduce the burden of remembering where everything lives
- ask guided questions when the user does not know where to start
- keep boundaries around sensitive actions

Val is not just a chatbot. The important pattern is operational continuity: Val helps convert scattered context into a practical next step, then learns what workflow is worth building around.

## 4. What Already Works

Current founder-beta capabilities include:

- Telegram surface
- guided onboarding
- Daily Operator demo flow
- Google Calendar read/create with confirmation where configured
- reminders/tasks basics
- folders/ideas basics
- documents/Caso Finca style support with boundaries
- conservative document/case summaries where source material exists
- bounded questions for legal/admin review without professional replacement claims

How to say it:

```text
It is already useful in controlled workflows. It is not universal yet, and that is why the first workflow matters.
```

## 5. What Does Not Exist Yet

Do not imply these are done:

- full autonomy
- persistent intake memory runtime
- polished universal LLM conversationality
- guaranteed production-grade reliability
- professional replacement claims
- public SaaS maturity
- universal integrations
- perfect OCR or perfect memory

Frank can say:

```text
Some of the Personal OS vision is designed, not fully implemented. The live thing today is narrower: choose one workflow, test it, and improve from real feedback.
```

## 6. Trust Boundaries

Trust is the product.

Boundaries to make explicit:

- user confirms actions
- no silent saving
- no fake consciousness
- no legal/medical/accounting replacement
- founder beta honesty
- no full autonomy claim
- no overpromising
- no hidden profiling
- no pressure if the workflow does not help

Good line:

```text
Val helps organize and prepare. She does not replace judgment, professionals, or your confirmation.
```

## 7. How Ale Can Help

Ale can help by:

- testing one real workflow
- giving honest feedback
- identifying where Val feels useful or awkward
- helping shape tone, onboarding, and packaging
- telling Frank where the explanation lands or loses her
- helping Frank think clearly as partner/right hand

Ask her for feedback on:

- the first 30 seconds of explanation
- whether Telegram feels easy enough
- whether the Daily Operator flow feels useful
- whether the boundaries feel trustworthy
- whether the $30 founder beta offer feels clear, fair, or premature

## 8. First Workflow Recommendation

Recommended first workflow for Ale:

```text
Organizar mi día
```

Why this is best first:

- it is easy to understand
- it does not require starting with sensitive documents
- it shows the Personal OS pattern quickly
- it can use scattered sources like WhatsApp, notes, calendar, tasks, screenshots, or memory
- it makes value visible in one week
- it opens later expansion into reminders, calendar, documents, follow-up, or ideas

One-week test:

1. Pick what currently gets scattered.
2. Define what the Daily Operator should include.
3. Use Telegram as the surface for one week.
4. Keep Google Calendar optional/confirmation-based.
5. Capture where Val helped, where she was awkward, and what should change.
6. Decide whether to continue, adjust, expand, or stop.

## 9. Founder-Beta Offer Framing

Frame it softly:

- around $30/month if useful after trial
- not selling a giant app
- not selling "AI that does everything"
- founder-beta gets closer attention and better conditions
- no pressure if it does not help

Suggested phrasing:

```text
If after a week this actually saves you friction, the personal founder-beta version is around $30/month. That is not a giant app subscription; it is early access with closer attention while we learn what workflows really help.
```

If it does not help:

```text
Then we do not force it. We either adjust the workflow or stop there.
```

## 10. Strategic Explanation

The strategic direction:

- build from one pain point
- prove value
- expand modularly
- keep trust first
- move toward a Personal OS that knows workflows, boundaries, and context

How to explain the Personal OS direction:

```text
The big idea is not a chatbot. It is a trust-first personal operating layer. But the way to build that honestly is not by claiming everything at once. We start with one workflow that hurts, prove value, then expand carefully.
```

Frank's internal anchor:

```text
Ale does not need the whole architecture first. She needs to feel the pattern: Val listens, narrows the chaos, proposes one workflow, keeps boundaries, and improves through feedback.
```
