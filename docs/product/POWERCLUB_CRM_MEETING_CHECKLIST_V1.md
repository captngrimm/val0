# POWERCLUB-CRM-BATTLE - Meeting Checklist V1

## Purpose

Run the PowerClub meeting without hunting through files or improvising scope, price, or promises.

## Before Meeting

- Open `docs/demo/powerclub_crm/index.html`.
- Open `docs/product/POWERCLUB_CRM_BATTLE_01D_LIVE_MEETING_SCRIPT.md`.
- Open `docs/product/POWERCLUB_CRM_DISCOVERY_QUESTIONNAIRE_V1.md`.
- Open `docs/product/POWERCLUB_CRM_EXECUTIVE_LEAVE_BEHIND_V1.md`.
- Keep `docs/product/POWERCLUB_CRM_PROPOSAL_PRICING_MODEL_V1.md` nearby, not on screen.
- Keep `docs/product/POWERCLUB_CRM_SCOPE_FREEZE_CHECKLIST_V1.md` nearby for next step.
- Confirm the demo still shows fake-data/no-production labels.
- Decide whether pricing will be discussed in this meeting or saved for follow-up.

## Opening

Say:

```text
Esto es una demo de piloto, no el sistema final. No esta conectado a datos reales de PowerClub, no automatiza WhatsApp, no maneja pagos y no promete reemplazar su CRM hoy.
```

Then frame:

```text
La oportunidad aqui es visibilidad: ver seguimiento atrasado, responsabilidad por asesor, desempeno por sucursal y oportunidades que todavia pueden protegerse.
```

## Demo Flow

1. Start on `Vista gerencial`.
2. Point to fake-data notice.
3. Show KPI strip.
4. Explain pain -> visibility -> action -> opportunity protected.
5. Show protected opportunity estimate as demo assumption.
6. Show advisor activity ranking and advisor result ranking.
7. Explain rankings as coaching, not punishment.
8. Show follow-up control: pending, overdue, aging.
9. Show stuck opportunities.
10. Show branch comparison.
11. Show source/channel performance.
12. Show Manager View Options.
13. Switch to `Vista asesores`.
14. Open one fake record and show next action/history.
15. Return to manager question: which view should be first in a real pilot?

## Questions To Ask

Use selected questions from `docs/product/POWERCLUB_CRM_DISCOVERY_QUESTIONNAIRE_V1.md`.

Priority questions:

- Which branch/process should be piloted first?
- How many advisors/users would be included?
- Where is follow-up most often lost?
- What does management need to see every morning?
- Which manager view matters most: follow-up, advisor performance, branch comparison, or recovery opportunities?
- What statuses and follow-up rules do you actually use?
- What should be explicitly out of scope for V1?
- Who approves budget and scope freeze?

## Pricing / Scope Discussion

Only discuss pricing after the pain and pilot scope are understood.

Safe phrasing:

```text
Yo separaria esto en setup/desarrollo, implementacion/training y mantenimiento mensual. Asi no prometemos cambios infinitos y podemos lanzar un piloto controlado.
```

Default anchor:

```text
Una base razonable para discutir seria $2,500 de setup y $1,000 mensuales por tres meses, ajustable segun alcance.
```

Use pricing ranges:

- Founder-friendly / reduced pilot.
- Recommended pilot.
- Corporate / expanded pilot.

Always pair price with scope freeze.

## What Not To Say

- "Esto ya esta conectado a PowerClub."
- "Esto usa datos reales."
- "WhatsApp entra en V1."
- "Pagos entran en V1."
- "Esto reemplaza su CRM desde hoy."
- "Cualquier reporte se puede agregar sin costo."
- "Los cambios son ilimitados."
- "Val ya opera PowerClub."
- "Val PowerClub Copilot viene incluido."

## How To Handle "Can It Also Do X?"

If X is inside current pilot:

```text
Si, eso esta dentro del alcance que estamos conversando. Lo ponemos en el checklist.
```

If X is useful but outside V1:

```text
Tiene sentido, pero lo pondria como fase dos o change request. Para que el piloto funcione, primero congelamos una vista util y medible.
```

If X is WhatsApp, payments, mobile app, full SaaS, SLA, or Copilot:

```text
Eso se puede evaluar despues, pero no lo venderia escondido dentro de V1. Tiene otro nivel de riesgo, soporte y precio.
```

## How To Close

Close with one of these:

```text
El siguiente paso seria discovery corto, congelar alcance y armar propuesta de piloto.
```

```text
Si esta direccion les hace sentido, la siguiente decision no es construir todo; es escoger el primer proceso/sucursal y la primera vista gerencial.
```

Ask:

- Who should attend discovery?
- Who approves scope?
- Who approves budget?
- What timeline matters?

## Post-Meeting Follow-Up

Send:

- Short recap.
- `docs/product/POWERCLUB_CRM_EXECUTIVE_LEAVE_BEHIND_V1.md`
- Tailored proposal draft only after discovery.

Do not send:

- Raw internal guidebook.
- Raw fake data requirements.
- Internal pricing guardrails.
- QA/polish notes.
- Full question bank unless requested.

## What To Send After

If early interest:

- Executive leave-behind.
- Short note asking for discovery slot.

If serious buying signal:

- Tailored client proposal draft.
- Scope freeze checklist.
- Pricing option recommendation.

If they ask for technical detail:

- Share selected dashboard/field excerpts, not the full internal packet.

## What To Checkpoint Internally

After the meeting, capture:

- Who attended.
- What pain mattered most.
- Which manager view they preferred.
- Which branch/process they mentioned.
- Budget/timeline signal.
- Objections.
- Red flags.
- Whether Copilot came up.
- Whether WhatsApp/payments/integrations came up.
- Recommended next lane.

## Capability Unlocked

- Frank can show the dashboard first instead of explaining abstractly.
- Frank can explain why each manager metric matters.
- Frank can offer package ranges without improvising.
- Frank can freeze scope before the client asks for everything.
- Frank can position Val PowerClub Copilot as future/add-on without overpromising.

## Guardrails

- Fake data only.
- No real PowerClub data.
- No backend promise.
- No payment integration.
- No WhatsApp automation.
- No production restart.
- No final production CRM promise.
- No unlimited customization.
- Val PowerClub Copilot remains future/add-on only unless separately scoped.
