# VAL0_VAL1_OFFER_ARCHITECTURE_V0

Purpose:
Separate the founder-beta offer into two clear sales routes without pretending there are two separate platforms.

Val0 and Val1 are offer routes on top of the same trust-first operating layer. The product is still founder-operated beta, Telegram-first, and workflow-specific.

---

## Why Two Offer Routes Exist

The current Karen MVP proves a personal/operator route:

- family/legal/admin continuity
- documents and case memory
- reminders, timeline, and Daily Operator
- a trusted assistant that helps one person organize a messy life workflow

The business opportunity also needs a separate route:

- workflow/process assessment
- implementation support
- company or team operations
- ongoing roadmap and automation planning

These are different buying contexts. A personal user does not buy like a business owner. A business owner needs a process map, scope, value case, and implementation lane.

The split is commercial and operational, not architectural. Do not fork the platform early.

---

## Shared Core

Both routes use the same core:

- Val0 Telegram assistant
- memory and source-of-truth rules
- client profile and workflow guards
- unknown-client protected workflow denial
- technical paste guard
- document/case/timeline patterns
- pending action confirmations
- Daily Operator snapshot pattern
- response safety envelope
- compile/audit/smoke discipline

Shared promise:

Val0 helps organize operational chaos with client-scoped memory, careful workflow boundaries, and explicit confirmation for sensitive actions.

Shared boundary:

No route should promise autonomous action, perfect OCR, full integrations, or self-serve SaaS.

## Val0 Personal / Individual

### Target User

Individuals or families with personal/admin continuity problems:

- family legal/admin matters
- documents spread across chats, folders, photos, and memory
- reminders and appointments
- personal projects
- caregiver/family coordination
- “I am lost, help me organize what comes next”

Karen is the first concrete pattern for this route.

### Current Capabilities

Val0 Personal can be demoed around:

- Telegram assistant
- notes, reminders, tasks, and ideas
- document inventory
- document/photo upload status
- case timeline
- Daily Operator read-only summary
- meeting/lawyer prep package
- calendar agenda/create/delete with confirmation
- client isolation and unknown-chat guardrails

### Use Cases

Good early use cases:

- organize a family/legal/admin case
- prepare for a lawyer/advisor meeting
- track documents and what still needs review
- create a timeline from notes and documents
- ask “what do I do today?” or “what is pending?”
- keep reminders and appointments from slipping

### Pricing Posture

Personal pricing can be lighter and more experimental than business pricing.

Possible founder-beta posture:

- first guided setup may be free, symbolic, or discounted for close testers
- small monthly beta fee once the workflow is actually useful
- pricing should reflect setup/support effort, not pretend this is self-serve software

Do not force the $300/month business package onto a personal user unless the support/workflow load justifies it.

### Demo Style

Demo one narrow workflow:

1. what Val0 can do today
2. document inventory/status
3. timeline
4. Daily Operator
5. meeting prep or missing-review checklist
6. explicit boundary: not legal advice, not perfect OCR

The demo should feel like relief and organization, not a feature tour.

### Boundaries

Val0 Personal is not:

- legal advice
- therapy
- full personal data vault
- perfect memory
- autonomous representative
- guaranteed OCR/photo reader
- public self-serve product

Sensitive documents require explicit comfort with beta storage.

## Val1 Business / Company

### Target Client

Small businesses, founders, operators, consultants, and professionals with workflow chaos:

- follow-ups spread across messages
- customer/vendor/admin tasks
- documents and checklists
- meetings and next actions
- recurring operational processes
- unclear automation priorities

Val1 is the business route: assessment, workflow mapping, implementation support, and ongoing operator package.

### $150 Initial Assessment

Purpose:
Map the business workflow before promising implementation.

Format:

- 45-60 minute assessment
- identify pain points
- map current tools and handoffs
- choose first 1-3 workflows
- define what Val0/Val1 can do now vs later
- document privacy boundaries

Deliverable:

- pain-point summary
- first workflow recommendation
- month 1 roadmap
- what can be built/adapted now
- what needs custom work
- what should be deferred

Client-facing line:

“The assessment tells us whether Val can help your actual workflow before we pretend to automate everything.”

### $300/Month Base Operator Package

Purpose:
Give a business a founder-operated AI implementation lane without selling unlimited custom development.

Includes:

- guided setup
- Telegram assistant access
- 1-3 active workflows
- workflow/process mapping
- client-specific roadmap
- weekly or biweekly check-in
- light refinement and maintenance
- AI implementation/operator support
- compile/audit/smoke discipline before changes

May include:

- document/admin tracking
- meeting prep
- customer/vendor follow-up tracking
- task and reminder flows
- simple list/inventory workflows
- status/readiness reporting
- future integration planning

Costs extra or requires separate scope:

- dashboards
- team/member permissions
- third-party integrations
- OCR/document automation beyond current limits
- custom data imports
- workflows with legal/financial/medical risk
- anything that requires strong uptime/SLA guarantees

### Sales Language

Plain version:

“Val1 is the business route for Val: we map one messy process, set up a focused operator workflow, and improve it with real usage.”

Sharper version:

“This is not a chatbot subscription. It is a founder-operated AI workflow implementation package.”

### Boundaries

Val1 Business is not:

- a complete CRM
- a complete ERP
- a public SaaS dashboard
- a no-code automation agency with unlimited scope
- an autonomous employee
- a system that mutates business data without confirmation
- a substitute for legal, accounting, medical, or financial professionals

## What Not To Do

- Do not present Val0 and Val1 as two separate platforms yet.
- Do not create two codebases, two brands, or two product architectures.
- Do not promise SaaS or self-serve onboarding.
- Do not promise autonomous actions.
- Do not promise OCR, DOCX extraction, or full document automation.
- Do not promise full integrations with WhatsApp, Gmail, CRMs, ERPs, calendars, or accounting tools.
- Do not imply that business clients get stronger privacy guarantees than the system actually has.
- Do not let business pricing create unlimited support expectations.
- Do not sell dashboards before evaluating prototype tools and data contracts.

## How To Talk About It In Founder-Beta Sales

Use one brand with two routes:

- Val0 Personal: personal/family/admin operator
- Val1 Business: business workflow/operator package

Both routes share:

- same trust-first operating layer
- same safety discipline
- same client isolation rules
- same beta honesty
- same confirmation-first action policy

Client-facing framing:

“Val has a personal route and a business route. The personal route helps with life/admin continuity. The business route starts with workflow assessment and implementation support. Same operating layer, different use case and scope.”

When someone asks “which one am I?”:

- If the pain is personal/family/admin continuity, start with Val0 Personal.
- If the pain is business process, team workflow, clients, vendors, revenue, or operations, start with Val1 Business.

---

## Decision Notes

### Offer Val0 Personal When

- the user is one person or family
- the workflow is personal/admin/legal/family continuity
- the main value is memory, organization, reminders, documents, and next steps
- pricing sensitivity is higher
- support can stay narrow and guided
- there is no need for team workflows or integrations

### Offer Val1 Business When

- the client is a business owner, founder, consultant, or operator
- the workflow affects revenue, customers, vendors, operations, or staff
- the first step should be assessment and process mapping
- there is a clear monthly value case
- the client expects implementation support
- future tools/integrations may matter

### If It Is Ambiguous

Start with the $150 assessment.

Use the assessment to decide:

- personal setup
- business package
- defer
- custom scope
- not a fit
