# OUTREACH_MESSAGE_TEMPLATES_V0

Purpose:
WhatsApp-friendly message templates for first real conversations about Val.

This is documentation only. It is not runtime copy, not a bot route, not a sales automation system, and not permission to activate a user or client. Use these messages as operator drafts before a human conversation.

Tone:
Spanish-first, warm, direct, human, no corporate sludge, no hype bullshit.

---

## 1. Core Positioning Rules

Use these rules before sending any outreach message:

- Val is not "a Telegram bot."
- Telegram is the first cockpit because it is practical for founder-beta use.
- Val is a personal/operator operating layer for continuity, memory, documents, reminders, workflows, decisions, and next actions.
- Val0 Personal is the $30/month founder personal plan.
- Val1 Business starts with a $150 assessment and may lead to a $300/month base operator package if there is fit.
- Do not promise magic memory.
- Do not promise autonomous actions.
- Do not promise unsupported OCR, photo, or DOCX reading.
- Do not imply legal, medical, accounting, tax, financial, or other professional advice.
- Do not imply self-serve setup.
- Do not imply that every module is enabled for every user.

Safe core line:

```text
Val empieza por Telegram, pero no es Telegram. Es una capa operativa en founder-beta para ayudarte a no perder el hilo de pendientes, documentos, decisiones y próximos pasos.
```

## 2. Message Templates

### A. Karen / Friends Founder Follow-Up

Use after a warm founder-user conversation with a trusted personal/family user. Keep it grateful and practical.

```text
Hola [nombre], gracias por sentarte conmigo a probar Val.

La idea esta primera semana no es probar todo. Mejor escogemos un flujo concreto que de verdad te quite peso: pendientes, documentos, recordatorios, una cronología, una lista o preparación para una reunión.

Como founder personal / Friends & Family, lo dejamos simple: $30/mes. Ese precio queda protegido aunque Val mejore con módulos reutilizables. Si de tu uso sale una mejora que también ayuda al producto, eso entra como aprendizaje y R&D.

Lo que sí separo es custom pesado: integraciones, urgencias, negocio/equipo, dashboards, soporte intensivo, migraciones, OCR garantizado o algo muy específico que no sea reusable. Eso lo hablamos aparte antes de prometerlo.

Para esta semana, probemos:
1. [workflow elegido]
2. 3-5 prompts simples
3. un check-in corto para ver qué ayudó, qué falló y qué se sintió confuso

Val no reemplaza criterio profesional, no promete leer todo perfecto y no toma acciones sola. Prefiero que diga la verdad cuando algo necesita revisión.
```

Short version:

```text
Gracias por ayudarme a probar Val. Para esta semana no probemos todo: escojamos un flujo real, lo usamos 3-5 veces y me dices qué ayudó, qué falló y qué se sintió raro. Founder personal queda en $30/mes; mejoras reutilizables incluidas, custom pesado aparte.
```

## 4. Do-Not-Send List

Do not send or imply:

- "Val will run your business."
- "Val remembers everything."
- "Val has infinite memory."
- "Val reads everything."
- "Val reads all photos and documents perfectly."
- "OCR is guaranteed."
- "DOCX extraction is automatic."
- "Val automates everything."
- "Val takes actions by itself."
- "Val replaces legal, medical, accounting, tax, or financial advice."
- "Val is a finished SaaS product."
- "You can self-serve onboarding today."
- "Telegram is the whole product."
- "Everything custom is included in $30."
- "Everything custom is included in $300."
- "Integrations are included."
- "Business/team/process work fits under the $30 personal plan."

Safer replacement:

```text
Val is founder-beta. We pick one focused workflow, keep limits clear, and put unsupported requests on the roadmap instead of pretending.
```

## 5. Operator Notes

- Personalize lightly: name, relationship, one likely workflow.
- Keep the first ask small.
- Do not send a huge manifesto.
- Do not include private facts in reusable outreach docs.
- Do not mention sensitive details unless the user already raised them in the same conversation and consent/scope are clear.
- If business, team, customer, vendor, revenue, process ownership, integrations, dashboards, SLA, or heavy support appears, route to Val1 assessment.
- If personal, family, admin, reminders, documents, lists, or continuity appears, route to Val0 founder path.
- If the request requires unsupported OCR/DOCX, autonomous actions, high-risk data, or professional conclusions, pause and scope carefully.
- After a promising conversation, create or update the appropriate roadmap or assessment summary instead of relying on memory.
