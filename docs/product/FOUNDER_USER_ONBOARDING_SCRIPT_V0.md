# FOUNDER_USER_ONBOARDING_SCRIPT_V0

Purpose:
Practical operator script for sitting with a founder user before activating or guiding a Val0 Personal workflow.

This is not a bot route, not self-serve onboarding, and not a product promise. It is a guided conversation aid for Frank/operator to set the relationship, choose one safe workflow, define week-1 success, and keep founder-beta boundaries clear.

Tone:
Spanish-first, warm, direct, founder relationship, visionary but grounded, no hype bullshit.

---

## 1. Who This Is For

Use this with:

- Karen/friends/family founder users
- early Val0 Personal users
- trusted personal users who may benefit from a guided founder-beta pilot

Do not use this as:

- a full Val1 Business assessment
- a self-serve onboarding script
- a promise that every module is enabled for every user
- a reason to upload sensitive documents without explicit consent

If the conversation becomes about a business process, multiple users, customers/vendors, urgent support, integrations, dashboards, team permissions, or operational responsibility, move toward a Val1 assessment instead of treating it as a $30 personal founder plan.

## 2. Conversation Structure

### Relationship Opening

Start with the relationship, not the software.

Goal:
Invite the person into a real founder-beta relationship: useful now, honest limits, feedback matters.

Operator prompt:

“Quiero enseñarte Val con calma, no venderte magia. Si te sirve en algo real, podemos probar un flujo pequeño esta semana y tú me dices qué ayuda, qué falla y qué se siente raro.”

### Full Val Vision

Explain that Telegram is the first cockpit, not the whole product.

Core idea:

Val is a personal/operator operating layer for memory, documents, reminders, decisions, workflows, and next actions.

Safe phrasing:

“Hoy empieza por Telegram porque es práctico, pero la idea no es quedarse en un chat. La visión es una capa operativa personal que te ayude a no perder el hilo de documentos, pendientes, decisiones y próximos pasos.”

### What Works Today

Name concrete capabilities without implying every user has every module enabled.

What can be described as real today:

- reminders, tasks, notes, and idea capture where enabled
- document inventory and upload/status tracking where enabled
- document readiness status, including OCR/manual-review limits
- timeline or chronology for a scoped personal/admin matter where enough context exists
- Daily Operator style summaries: what is pending, what is next, what needs review
- meeting or lawyer-prep checklists when enough context has been registered
- calendar flows with explicit confirmation where connected and enabled

Useful line:

“Lo que ya hace bien es ayudarte a no perder el hilo: qué hay pendiente, qué documentos existen, qué falta revisar, qué sigue hoy y cuándo algo necesita confirmación.”

### What Is Not Ready Yet

Be plain. Trust comes from not pretending.

Say:

- Val is founder-beta, not finished public SaaS.
- Val does not have magic or infinite memory.
- OCR/photo reading and DOCX extraction are not guaranteed.
- Val does not take sensitive actions by itself.
- Val does not replace lawyers, doctors, accountants, tax advisors, or professional reviewers.
- Val is not self-serve onboarding yet.

Useful line:

“Prefiero que Val diga ‘esto está guardado pero no leído’ antes que fingir que entendió algo que no pudo leer.”

### Founder Pact

Explain what the user gives and what they get.

Founder user gives:

- real usage
- honest feedback
- examples of what helped or failed
- permission to generalize reusable patterns into product improvements
- patience with beta limits

Founder relationship gives:

- favorable personal founder pricing
- guided setup around one useful workflow
- reusable improvements included as Val improves
- a clearer individual roadmap

### $30 Founder Personal Pricing

Use direct language.

“Para Friends & Family / founder personal, el plan es $30 al mes para uso personal. Ese precio no sube solo porque Val mejore con capacidades reutilizables.”

Clarify:

- This is a personal founder plan, not a custom software retainer.
- The price is protected as reusable Val0 Personal modules improve.
- Product improvements that can help other users can be treated as product R&D.

### Custom / Heavy Work Boundaries

Keep the boundary warm but firm.

Separate scope if the request becomes:

- urgent custom development
- unlimited human support
- business/team workflow
- complex integration
- dashboard or portal
- migration or bulk cleanup
- guaranteed OCR/photo/DOCX automation
- sensitive or high-risk automation
- legal, medical, accounting, tax, or financial advice

Useful line:

“Quiero ser generoso con los primeros, pero también cuidar que Val sea sostenible. Si algo se vuelve custom grande, integración, urgencia, negocio/equipo o soporte pesado, lo hablamos aparte antes de prometerlo.”

### Pick One Workflow

Do not invite the user to test everything.

Ask:

- “Qué te pesa esta semana?”
- “Qué se te pierde más fácil?”
- “Qué te daría alivio si Val lo organizara un poco?”
- “Qué sería útil repetir dos o tres veces esta semana?”

Choose one:

- legal/admin document case
- personal reminders/admin continuity
- grocery/list workflow
- meeting prep / next actions

### Define Week-1 Success

Make success concrete.

Examples:

- “Usaste Val 3 veces en el flujo elegido.”
- “Val te recordó o te mostró un pendiente que normalmente se te pierde.”
- “Terminaste la semana con una lista más clara de próximos pasos.”
- “Sabemos qué parte sirve, qué parte confunde y qué habría que mejorar.”

### Feedback Loop

Set cadence before ending.

Suggested:

- day 1: setup and first prompts
- day 3 or 4: quick check-in
- day 7: review and decide keep / adjust / pause / rescope

Ask for feedback in concrete terms:

- what helped
- what failed
- what felt stiff or confusing
- what they would use again tomorrow
- what should not be automated

## 3. Verbal Script In Spanish

Use this naturally. Do not recite it like a corporate pitch.

“Mira, estoy construyendo esto que se llama Val. Hoy lo usas por Telegram porque es el lugar más fácil para empezar: ya lo tienes en la mano, puedes mandar notas, recordatorios, documentos o preguntas rápido. Pero no quiero venderlo como un bot de Telegram, porque la visión es más grande.

La idea es que Val sea una capa operativa personal. Algo que te ayude a organizar memoria, documentos, recordatorios, decisiones y próximos pasos. Como una especie de sistema operativo personal, pero conversacional y práctico: no para hacerlo todo por ti, sino para que no se te pierda el hilo.

Ahora mismo está en founder-beta. Eso significa que ya puede ayudar en flujos concretos, pero no voy a prometer magia. Puede servir para recordatorios, tareas, documentos, cronologías, Daily Operator, preparar una reunión, o tener más claro qué falta revisar. Pero no significa que lea todo perfecto, ni que tenga memoria infinita, ni que tome acciones sola.

Con documentos prefiero decir la verdad: si algo quedó guardado pero no leído, Val debe decir eso. Si una foto necesita OCR o revisión manual, también. Y si algo es legal, médico, contable o financiero, Val ayuda a organizar información; la decisión profesional la toma la persona profesional.

Lo que quiero con Friends & Family / founder users es una relación honesta: si te sirve, te dejo un plan personal de $30 al mes. Ese precio no sube solo porque Val mejore con módulos reutilizables. Si de tu uso sale una mejora que ayuda al producto, eso entra como aprendizaje y R&D.

Lo que sí separo es custom pesado: integraciones, urgencias, dashboards, negocio/equipo, soporte intensivo o algo muy específico que no sea reusable. Eso lo hablaríamos aparte antes de prometerlo.

Para empezar no quiero que pruebes todo. Mejor escogemos un workflow pequeño y real para esta semana. Algo que de verdad te pese: recordatorios, documentos, una lista, una reunión, próximos pasos. Definimos qué sería éxito en una semana y me dices qué te alivió, qué falló y qué se sintió torpe. Ahí es donde Val mejora de verdad.”

Short version:

“Val empieza en Telegram, pero no es Telegram. Es una capa operativa personal en founder-beta. Si te ayuda en un flujo real, entras como founder user: $30/mes para uso personal, mejoras reutilizables incluidas, custom pesado aparte, y beta honesta sin prometer magia.”

## 4. Week-1 Guided Pilot Checklist

Use this before the user starts.

### Scope

- [ ] User is a personal founder user, not a business/team workflow.
- [ ] One workflow is selected.
- [ ] The workflow is safe for founder-beta.
- [ ] The workflow does not require unsupported integrations or urgent reliability.

### Privacy Boundaries

- [ ] User understands Val0 is founder-beta.
- [ ] User explicitly agrees what information can be sent.
- [ ] User knows what should not be sent yet.
- [ ] Sensitive documents are not uploaded unless consent and beta-storage limits are clear.
- [ ] Professional-advice boundary is stated if relevant.

### What They Can Send / Upload

Define allowed inputs for the week:

- text notes
- reminder requests
- task/admin notes
- safe demo documents
- non-sensitive document names or summaries
- photos/documents only if the pilot explicitly includes them and limits are understood

Define off-limits inputs:

- highly sensitive documents without explicit consent
- bulk document dumps
- urgent legal/medical/financial instructions
- passwords, tokens, IDs, or private access credentials
- business/team/customer data unless scoped as Val1

### Test Prompts

Pick 3-5 prompts for the week. Keep them tied to the selected workflow.

Examples:

- “Val, recuérdame mañana a las 9 revisar [pendiente].”
- “Val, guarda esta nota: [nota corta].”
- “Val, qué tengo pendiente?”
- “Val, qué hago hoy?”
- “Val, qué falta revisar antes de [reunión/cita]?”

### Success Criteria

Choose 2-3:

- [ ] User tried Val at least 3 times.
- [ ] Val helped recover or organize one real pending item.
- [ ] User can name one thing that felt useful.
- [ ] User can name one failure or confusion.
- [ ] Next workflow improvement is clear.
- [ ] No unsafe promise or unsupported feature was implied.

### Feedback Cadence

- [ ] Day 1: setup / first prompt.
- [ ] Day 3 or 4: quick feedback check.
- [ ] Day 7: review outcome.

Week-1 review questions:

- “Qué te alivió?”
- “Qué no entendió?”
- “Qué se sintió torpe?”
- “Qué usarías otra vez?”
- “Qué no quieres que Val haga?”
- “Seguimos, ajustamos, pausamos o cambiamos de workflow?”

### What Not To Test Yet

- self-serve onboarding
- “use Val for everything”
- large document migrations
- perfect OCR/photo reading
- automatic DOCX understanding
- autonomous actions
- professional conclusions
- business/team workflows under the $30 personal plan
- dashboards, portals, or custom integrations

## 5. Example Pilot Tracks

### A. Karen-Style Legal/Admin Document Case

Use when:
The user has a personal/family/admin matter with documents, dates, reminders, and professional prep.

Week-1 workflow:
Organize what exists, what is pending, and what needs review before the next conversation or appointment.

Good prompts:

- “Val, guarda esta nota del caso: [hecho concreto].”
- “Val, qué documentos tengo registrados?”
- “Val, qué falta revisar antes de hablar con la abogada?”
- “Val, ordéname la cronología del caso.”
- “Val, qué hago hoy?”

Success:

- A short pending/review list exists.
- The user knows which items are documents, reminders, or questions.
- Val does not provide legal advice.
- Documents/photos that need OCR or manual review are labeled honestly.

Boundary:
Val organizes information and questions; a lawyer or professional reviewer gives legal conclusions.

### B. Personal Reminders / Admin Continuity

Use when:
The user mainly loses tasks, appointments, errands, or small admin follow-ups.

Week-1 workflow:
Capture reminders and ask Val what is pending today.

Good prompts:

- “Val, recuérdame mañana a las 10 [acción].”
- “Val, guarda esta tarea: [tarea].”
- “Val, qué tengo pendiente?”
- “Val, qué hago hoy?”
- “Val, ya hice [tarea].”

Success:

- User captures 3-5 real reminders/tasks.
- At least one Daily Operator or pending check is useful.
- Stale or overdue items are reviewed honestly, not presented as fresh today work.

Boundary:
No urgent/SLA promise. Sensitive actions still require confirmation where supported.

### C. Grocery / List Workflow

Use when:
The user wants lightweight list continuity, not full inventory automation.

Week-1 workflow:
Capture simple list items manually and review before shopping.

Good prompts:

- “Val, agrega leche a la lista del súper.”
- “Val, agrega arroz y huevos a la lista.”
- “Val, qué tengo en la lista del súper?”
- “Val, borra/completa [item] si el flow lo soporta.”
- “Val, guarda esta idea para la lista recurrente: [idea].”

Success:

- User can add and retrieve list items.
- The list feels easier than remembering manually.
- Gaps become reusable product ideas.

Boundary:
No barcode/photo/OCR grocery automation promised unless explicitly built and enabled.

### D. Meeting Prep / Next Actions

Use when:
The user has a meeting, call, appointment, or conversation and wants less mental clutter.

Week-1 workflow:
Capture context, questions, and next actions before and after the meeting.

Good prompts:

- “Val, guarda esta nota para la reunión: [nota].”
- “Val, recuérdame preparar la reunión el [día/hora].”
- “Val, qué preguntas debo llevar a la reunión?”
- “Val, resume mis pendientes para esta cita.”
- “Val, después de la reunión guarda estos próximos pasos: [pasos].”

Success:

- User enters the meeting with clearer questions.
- Follow-ups are captured after the meeting.
- At least one next action is not lost.

Boundary:
Val prepares and organizes; it does not make professional decisions or negotiate on the user’s behalf.

## 7. Do-Not-Say List

Do not say:

- “Es un bot de Telegram.”
- “Telegram es el producto completo.”
- “Val tiene memoria infinita.”
- “Val recuerda todo.”
- “Val lee todos tus documentos y fotos perfecto.”
- “OCR está garantizado.”
- “DOCX funciona automático.”
- “Val toma acciones sola.”
- “Val puede manejar todo por ti.”
- “Val reemplaza a tu abogado, contador, médico, asesor financiero o profesional.”
- “Puedes hacer onboarding solo.”
- “Mándale todo y Val lo organiza.”
- “Todo custom está incluido en $30.”
- “Nunca habrá límites.”

Safer replacements:

- “Telegram es el primer cockpit.”
- “Val es una capa operativa personal en founder-beta.”
- “La memoria se está estructurando con fuentes, contexto y límites.”
- “Val ayuda a organizar; las decisiones profesionales siguen con profesionales.”
- “Si algo no está leído, Val debe decirlo.”
- “Las acciones sensibles requieren confirmación.”
- “El plan founder incluye mejoras reutilizables, no custom ilimitado.”
- “Empezamos con un workflow concreto y guiado.”

## 8. Operator Notes

After the conversation:

- Write short notes from the call.
- Record the chosen week-1 workflow.
- Record privacy boundaries and what the user agreed they can send/upload.
- Define or update the individual founder roadmap.
- Identify reusable module ideas separately from one-off custom requests.
- Mark what is manual, what works now, and what is roadmap.
- Do not store sensitive information without explicit consent.
- Do not add a second hardcoded client or client-specific literals to reusable product docs/code.
- Do not activate workflows that are not ready or not scoped.
- Escalate business/team workflows to a Val1 assessment.

Useful roadmap fields:

- user type: personal founder / friends-family / Val1 candidate
- selected workflow
- week-1 success criteria
- enabled modules
- known limits
- privacy boundaries
- feedback cadence
- reusable product ideas
- custom/out-of-scope requests

Operating principle:

Founder onboarding should make the user feel invited and protected, not pushed into a funnel. The promise is useful now for one focused workflow, honest about limits, and built toward a larger personal operating layer.
