# VAL0 FOUNDER-BETA CANDIDATES

## Selection rules

Good first testers:
- use Telegram or are willing to use it
- have real reminders/tasks/notes pain
- can give honest feedback
- will tolerate beta rough edges
- are reachable for guided onboarding

Avoid first:
- people who expect perfection
- people who need enterprise polish
- people who will judge the whole thing from one bug
- people who need legal/medical/financial certainty
