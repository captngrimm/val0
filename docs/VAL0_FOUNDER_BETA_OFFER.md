# VAL0 FOUNDER-BETA OFFER

## Offer
Val0 Founder Beta — $30/month

## Promise
Val0 is a Telegram-based personal operator that helps users stay on top of reminders, notes, documents, calendar items, and daily priorities through conversation.

## Founder-beta framing
This is an early guided beta, not a polished mass-market app.

The goal is to help real users reduce mental friction, remember what matters, and give feedback while Val0 improves.

## What Val0 can help with now
- reminders
- notes
- daily priorities
- basic agenda/tomorrow checks
- simple document/email workflows
- voice mode
- bug/feedback/idea reporting
- guided conversational support

## What Val0 does NOT promise yet
- perfect memory
- full autonomy
- instant response every time
- flawless calendar automation
- replacing a human assistant
- enterprise dashboard
- public-launch polish

## Who this is for
- busy professionals
- solopreneurs
- people who forget tasks
- people who live in Telegram
- people willing to test and give feedback

## Founder-beta terms
- $30/month
- guided onboarding
- feedback expected
- bugs may happen
- early users help shape the product

## Success criteria
A founder-beta user should feel:
- less scattered
- more supported
- more likely to remember what matters
- comfortable reporting bugs, ideas, and confusing moments
