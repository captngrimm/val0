## Core positioning

Val is a personalized AI operator with memory, follow-up, and practical workflows.

Val is not just generic chat.

Val is configured around a person, family, or business process so it can help remember, organize, track, and follow up on things that matter.

Simple version:
Val helps you stop losing track of important things.

Stronger version:
Val is a personal/business operator that starts with simple useful workflows and grows with you.

## What Val does not fully do yet

Do not promise these as reliable in founder-beta:

- Full ChatGPT-level open conversation
- Perfect voice transcription
- Perfect OCR/photo document reading
- Barcode lookup
- Nutrition facts parsing
- Fully automated business operations
- Full CRM/ERP replacement
- Legal, medical, or financial professional advice
- Autonomous execution without human review
- Integrations with every platform

Safe framing:
“These are roadmap items, not current promises.”

## How Val grows with the client

Val starts small.

The first month focuses on 1-3 useful workflows.

As the client uses Val:
1. ideas are captured
2. needs are classified
3. existing capabilities are reused when possible
4. new capabilities enter the roadmap
5. unsafe or too-large requests are deferred
6. useful patterns can become platform capabilities

Client-facing phrase:
“Val grows with you. We start with the things that help immediately, then build based on how you actually use it.”

Internal rule:
Do not let “growth” become an unlimited promise.

## Founder-beta process

### Step 1 — Intro call / assessment

Goal:
Understand the person or business and identify first useful workflows.

Questions:
- What are you currently forgetting or losing track of?
- What process wastes time?
- What do you manage through WhatsApp, notes, screenshots, Excel, or memory?
- What would feel useful this week?
- What should Val avoid storing or touching?

Output:
- primary use case
- first pain point
- first 1-3 workflows
- limits / deferred items
- next action

### Step 2 — Setup

Use:
- client template generator
- CLIENT_PROFILE
- CLIENT_STATUS
- CLIENT_ROADMAP
- CLIENT_IDEAS
- CLIENT_CAPABILITIES

Output:
- client folder
- initial roadmap
- initial capabilities
- clear Week 1 target

### Step 3 — First workflows

Examples:
- list / inventory / grocery
- meeting prep
- document checklist
- reminders
- ideas and roadmap
- simple status summary

### Step 4 — Review and expand

After real usage:
- what worked?
- what confused the client?
- what should be next?
- what should be deferred?
- what is reusable for platform?

## Client-facing short explanation

Val is a personalized AI operator that helps you remember, organize, and follow up on important things.

It can start with simple workflows like lists, reminders, documents, meeting prep, ideas, and status tracking.

The difference is that Val is configured around your actual life or business. As you use it, we capture what you need, update your roadmap, and activate more capabilities over time.

It will not do everything on day one. The founder-beta is about starting with the most useful workflows first and growing from there.

## WhatsApp version — business client

Te cuento la idea en versión corta.

Estoy montando Val como una asistente operativa con memoria para negocios/personas. La idea no es venderte “un chatbot genérico”, sino ayudarte a mapear tus procesos y montar una Val inicial que te ayude con cosas concretas: pendientes, seguimiento, documentos, listas, clientes/proveedores, reuniones, ideas y próximos pasos.

El primer paso sería una evaluación corta: vemos qué proceso te quita más tiempo o se te desordena más, definimos 1-3 workflows para el primer mes, y armamos un roadmap realista.

Si ya tenemos una capacidad parecida construida, se adapta. Si no, se clasifica para roadmap. La idea es que el sistema crezca contigo, sin prometer magia de golpe.

Founder-beta significa: útil desde temprano, pero todavía en construcción controlada.

Short answer:
“ChatGPT es genérico. Val se configura alrededor de tu contexto, tus procesos, tu roadmap y tus pendientes. La diferencia no es solo conversar; es memoria estructurada, seguimiento y operación sobre tu mundo concreto.”

Expanded:
“Puedes usar ChatGPT para muchas cosas. Val busca ser una capa más personalizada: guarda contexto por cliente, mantiene roadmap, recuerda ideas, organiza procesos, y convierte lo que usas en capacidades repetibles. No compite solo por inteligencia; compite por continuidad y operación.”

## Internal sales guardrails

Never say:
- “Val can do everything.”
- “Val replaces your lawyer/accountant/doctor.”
- “Val will automate your whole business immediately.”
- “This is fully ready for the public.”
- “No errors.”

Prefer:
- “Founder-beta.”
- “Focused first workflows.”
- “Roadmap.”
- “Human review.”
- “We start small and grow.”
- “This is available now / this is planned / this is deferred.”
